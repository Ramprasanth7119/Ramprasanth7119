I'm a full-stack engineer who enjoys turning ambiguous product ideas into **fast, reliable, and visually refined software**. My comfort zone spans the entire stack — designing resilient APIs with **Java & Spring Boot**, building expressive interfaces with **React & Next.js**, and modeling systems that scale gracefully under real-world load.

- 🛠️ **What I build** — production web platforms, internal tools, and interactive front-end experiences that feel intentional, not templated.
- 💡 **What I enjoy** — the moment a system design finally clicks, and the moment a UI animation feels *just* right.
- 🧠 **My engineering philosophy** — simplicity is a feature; every abstraction has to earn its place.
- 🚀 **Where I'm headed** — deepening my expertise in distributed systems, cloud-native architecture, and applied AI, while shipping polished, real-world products.
